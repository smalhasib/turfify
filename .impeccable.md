# Design Context

This document is the source of truth for the visual design language of Turfify.
Read this before making any UI/visual decision.

## Users

- **Primary persona:** Men aged 18–35 in Bangladesh booking turf time with their friends for evening football (most common: 7-a-side at 6pm–10pm).
- **Use contexts (devices and moods):**
  1. Mid-day at the office on a phone, slack-checking with one hand, picking a slot for tonight.
  2. Evening from the sofa on a phone or laptop, comparing a couple of slots before paying.
  3. Right before the match — group chat already moving, urgency high, fat thumbs, on transit.
- **Job to be done:** Lock a turf slot for the friend group, fast, with confidence the booking is real.
- **Emotional goals:** *Confidence* (this booking is sealed) + *hype* (we're playing tonight).
- **Anti-emotions:** Anxiety, uncertainty, "did the payment actually go through?" feeling.

## Brand Personality

- **Three words:** Energetic. Local. No-nonsense.
- **Voice:** Crisp, direct, slightly informal. No marketing buzz. No exclamation marks. Talks like a captain at a Sunday morning kickoff: short sentences, instructions, the occasional dry humour.
- **Anti-references:**
  - Bangladeshi government portals — busy, dense, distrust-by-default.
  - Generic SaaS landing pages — vague gradients, "boost your productivity" copy, stock photography.

## Aesthetic Direction

**Concept:** Stadium under floodlights at evening kickoff.

- **Theme:** Auto (system preference). Both modes are first-class:
  - **Light mode** (daytime / office context): warm cream surfaces, deep grass-green ink, amber for action.
  - **Dark mode** (evening / pre-match context): midnight ground, grass-green accents, amber for action. Reads like a stadium broadcast graphic.
- **Color anchor:** Real-grass green (not Tailwind default). Sodium-vapor amber as the only accent. Neutrals tinted toward the green hue so brand and UI surfaces feel of one cloth.
- **Typography:** Bold condensed display for moments of impact (venue name, slot time, CTA), clean grotesque for everything else. Stadium-signage rhythm.
- **Layout:** Generous whitespace, decisive hierarchy, asymmetric compositions where useful. Cards used sparingly. Never nest cards.
- **Forbidden:**
  - Purple-blue gradients, cyan-on-dark, neon glow accents (AI default palette).
  - Glassmorphism / blurred translucent panels used decoratively.
  - Gradient text (`background-clip: text`).
  - Side-stripe colored borders on cards (`border-left: 4px solid …` and friends).
  - Generic rounded-rectangle cards with drop shadows.
  - Centered everything. Default to left-aligned with deliberate asymmetry.

## Color Tokens

All colors specified in OKLCH. Defined as CSS custom properties at `:root` and `[data-theme="dark"]`.

### Brand
- **`--brand-grass`**: `oklch(0.55 0.16 145)` — primary grass green. Used for headings on brand surfaces, brand wordmark, secondary buttons.
- **`--brand-grass-deep`**: `oklch(0.32 0.10 145)` — darker grass for high-contrast text on cream / for backgrounds in dark mode.
- **`--accent-flood`**: `oklch(0.74 0.17 70)` — sodium-vapor amber. The single hype color. Reserved for: primary booking CTAs, live indicators, slot-lock countdowns, payment confirmation.
- **`--accent-flood-deep`**: `oklch(0.55 0.16 65)` — darker amber for hover/active states.

### Light mode neutrals (tinted toward 145°)
- **`--bg`**: `oklch(0.985 0.005 95)` — cream-tinted page background.
- **`--surface`**: `oklch(0.97 0.008 145)` — slightly green-tinted card / panel surface.
- **`--surface-2`**: `oklch(0.93 0.010 145)` — second-level surface for nested elements.
- **`--ink`**: `oklch(0.20 0.020 145)` — primary text. Tinted toward brand green so neutrals feel cohesive.
- **`--ink-soft`**: `oklch(0.45 0.015 145)` — secondary text, captions.
- **`--ink-mute`**: `oklch(0.62 0.010 145)` — placeholder text, disabled states.
- **`--rule`**: `oklch(0.88 0.010 145)` — dividers, hairlines, input borders at rest.
- **`--rule-strong`**: `oklch(0.75 0.012 145)` — input borders on focus, button outlines.

### Dark mode neutrals (tinted toward 240° to evoke stadium night sky)
- **`--bg`**: `oklch(0.16 0.014 240)` — midnight pitch.
- **`--surface`**: `oklch(0.21 0.016 240)` — surface card.
- **`--surface-2`**: `oklch(0.27 0.018 240)`.
- **`--ink`**: `oklch(0.96 0.010 95)` — slightly warm white for legibility against the cool background.
- **`--ink-soft`**: `oklch(0.78 0.012 95)`.
- **`--ink-mute`**: `oklch(0.58 0.010 95)`.
- **`--rule`**: `oklch(0.32 0.014 240)`.
- **`--rule-strong`**: `oklch(0.42 0.016 240)`.

### State colors
- **`--success`**: `oklch(0.62 0.14 150)` — slightly bluer green than brand-grass to read as "confirmed" without confusing brand.
- **`--danger`**: `oklch(0.58 0.20 27)` — clay red, never pure red. Used only for destructive confirms and hard errors.
- **`--warn`**: shares `--accent-flood` because amber already plays this role.

## Typography

- **Display family:** **Big Shoulders Display** (Google Fonts, variable, weights 100–900). Used for: H1, H2, page-level numbers (e.g., big slot times), brand wordmark.
- **Body family:** **Hanken Grotesk** (Google Fonts, variable, weights 100–900). Used for: H3+, body text, buttons, form labels, all UI chrome.
- **Sizing:** Modular scale, 1.25 ratio, 5 steps. Headings use `clamp()` for marketing pages. Product UI uses fixed `rem` sizes for predictable density.
- **Tracking:**
  - Big Shoulders Display: `letter-spacing: -0.01em` at large sizes, `0.04em` at small/all-caps labels.
  - Hanken Grotesk: default tracking, `-0.005em` for body at large sizes.
- **Line height:** 1.05 for display headings, 1.5 for body, 1.25 for headings near body text.
- **Caps:** Display can be set in uppercase for slot times, scoreboard-style. Never set body text in uppercase.

## Spacing

4pt scale, semantic tokens:

- `--space-3xs` 4px
- `--space-2xs` 8px
- `--space-xs` 12px
- `--space-sm` 16px
- `--space-md` 24px
- `--space-lg` 32px
- `--space-xl` 48px
- `--space-2xl` 64px
- `--space-3xl` 96px

Use `gap` over margins for sibling spacing. Vary spacing aggressively for hierarchy — a CTA gets more space above it, related groups get less between them.

## Radii

- `--radius-xs` 4px (input fields, tags)
- `--radius-sm` 8px (buttons, small cards)
- `--radius-md` 12px (cards, modals)
- `--radius-lg` 20px (large brand surfaces, hero panels)

Used sparingly. Sharp corners (`0`) are valid for industrial/scoreboard moments.

## Motion

- Standard ease: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (ease-out-quart) — exponential deceleration, feels physical.
- Snappy ease: `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` for interactive feedback (button press, slot select).
- No bounce or elastic easing.
- Animate `transform`, `opacity`, and `grid-template-rows` for height transitions only.
- Stagger reveals at 30–60ms intervals on page load. Cap at 5 staggered children.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: disable transforms, keep opacity changes only.

## Imagery

- No stock photos.
- If photography is used, prefer authentic BD turf images (artificial-grass close-up, floodlight haze, players mid-action). Phase 13+ work; not in MVP.
- Iconography: linear / outlined, 1.5px stroke, 24px grid. Avoid filled icons except for status indicators (live dot, success check).

## Touch Targets & Accessibility

- Minimum interactive area: 44×44 CSS px (matches both Apple HIG and Material). Even on web, treat this as a hard floor.
- Focus rings: 2px solid `--accent-flood-deep`, offset 2px. Never `outline: none` without an alternative ring.
- Color contrast: meet WCAG AA for body text on every surface in both themes. Verify with a tool, not by eye.
- Form errors: text + icon, never color alone.

## Design Principles (the lived rules)

1. **Floodlit clarity** — high contrast, generous whitespace, decisive type. Govt-portal density is the enemy.
2. **Stadium signage hierarchy** — display type for venue/time/CTA; body for everything else. No precious serifs.
3. **Hype is rationed** — amber accent only on booking CTAs, "X minutes to lock", confirmation badges. Overuse kills it.
4. **Local without folkloric** — no red-green flag clichés, no cricket-bat motifs. Modern athletic; BD-aware through copy + `+880` phone format, not ornament.
5. **Touch-target discipline** — every clickable element ≥ 44px tall. Men check this site on the bus, with gloves in winter, with sweat on the screen post-match.
6. **No-nonsense copy** — short sentences. Verbs. No marketing language. The phrase "elevate your turf experience" is a fireable offence.
